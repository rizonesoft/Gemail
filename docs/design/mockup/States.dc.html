Rules
  • Light gets darker as you press; dark gets lighter on hover and darker on press for fills, and lighter for text, so text never drops below 4.5:1.
  • White on every fill state passes: 6.7:1 at rest, 5.4:1 on hover, 11.7:1 pressed in the dark theme.
  • Dark link hover is 6.8:1 and pressed is 7.8:1 on the pane background.
  • Outlined, icon, tab and dropdown controls reuse existing tokens: hover is surface, pressed is surfaceSunken in light and border in dark. No new greys.
  • Focus is a 2 px brand ring with a 2 px offset in both themes (5.4:1 light, 5.9:1 dark against the pane), never colour alone; a focused text input also takes the 2 px brand underline.
  • Disabled controls sit on surfaceSunken with disabledText and no border; they are not required to meet contrast.

2 · Ribbon and menu icons redrawn for 16 px

At 16 px the 32 px drawing turns to mush: the badge is five pixels wide. The redraw keeps the document base but drops to one text line, and grows the badge to nine pixels with a one-pixel white ring and a heavier glyph. 32 and 80 px keep the existing drawing.
