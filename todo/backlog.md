# Gemail -- Backlog

Ideas worth keeping that are not planned work. Nothing here is a section: an entry has no Implementation Order row, no phase, no checklist, and no owner, so no runner ever picks one up, `plan --check` never counts one, and nothing may defer work to one. The format spec, the admission test, and the budget are in [`README.md`](./README.md) under "The budget and the backlog"; the cap is `backlog_cap` in [`budget.json`](./budget.json).

**Entry grammar.** One line per entry: the id in brackets, the title, then ` -- key: value` fields. Required: `source` (one key token: a legacy key, a finding ID, or the `-> SOURCE:` key the section would carry), `added` (YYYY-MM-DD), `why deferred`, and `promote when`. Optional: `app`, `summary`, and `needs` (full `DNN TNN §N` refs to live sections, or other backlog ids). Ids are `B-NNN`, taken in order and never reused. `validate` refuses a malformed line, a duplicate id or source, an entry whose source a live section already carries, a dead or short section ref, and a count over the cap.

**Promotion** is `add-todo` on the entry (with its admission test, Origin line, and per-run cap when a campaign promotes it on its own): the promoting commit writes the real section (carrying this entry's source as its `-> SOURCE:` line) and deletes the entry. **Triage** is `groom-plan`: merge duplicates, drop stale entries with a one-line reason in the commit, and promote what has come due.

**History.** Started 2026-09-27 at the port from Photon. The same day every open item of the legacy root `TODO.md` was migrated and that file was deleted: items a planned section needs became checklist items or sections, and the rest are the entries below (`source: legacy-todo-*`) beside the operator's own deferrals (`source: operator-*`). Two legacy items were dropped rather than kept: telemetry opt-in (operator decision 2026-09-27: no telemetry) and encrypting the stored key with a device fingerprint (a key the page can decrypt is readable by anything that can read the page's storage; `D04 T01 §4` keeps it device-only instead).
